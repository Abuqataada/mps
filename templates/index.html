FAQs

Daily Interest Calculation

Our micro mutual funds generate 0.5% interest per working day (Monday to Friday). This means your investment grows by 0.5% each business day, excluding weekends and public holidays. For example, if you invest ₦100,000, you'll earn ₦500 in interest on the first working day, and this compounds daily throughout your investment period.

Profit Withdrawal Schedule

You can withdraw your accumulated profits after every 20 working days from your initial investment date. This means approximately every 4 weeks (excluding weekends). The system automatically calculates when your profits mature and makes them available for withdrawal in your dashboard. Your principal investment remains intact and continues to generate daily interest.

Investment Requirements

The minimum investment amount for our micro mutual funds is ₦5,000. There's no maximum limit - you can invest as much as you want. For real estate investments, the minimum is ₦500,000 with guaranteed 20% annual returns. We've designed our micro funds to be accessible to everyone while providing substantial returns.

Investment Security

Your investment is highly secure through multiple layers of protection: diversified investment portfolios, regulatory compliance, secure banking partnerships, and insurance coverage. We maintain transparent operations and regular audits. All transactions are encrypted and monitored 24/7. Your principal investment is protected while generating consistent returns.

Payment Options

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience: Paystack integration for card payments, direct bank transfers, USSD payments, and automated account funding. All payments are processed securely and reflected instantly in your account. Withdrawals are processed within 24-48 hours directly to your registered bank account.

Multiple Investment Options

Absolutely! You can simultaneously invest in our micro mutual funds, real estate projects, and community finance services. Many of our clients diversify their portfolios across different services to maximize returns and minimize risk. Your dashboard provides a comprehensive view of all your investments and their performance in one place.

Community Support Programs

We offer monthly community donation options at ₦250, ₦500, or ₦1,000 levels. These donations support community development projects, welfare programs, and educational initiatives. You can choose one-time donations or set up recurring monthly contributions. All community donations are transparently managed and impact reports are shared regularly with contributors.

Investment Tracking

You can track your investment performance in real-time through your personalized dashboard. It shows your current balance, daily interest earned, total profits, available withdrawals, and investment growth charts. You'll also receive weekly performance reports via email and can set up custom alerts for important milestones and profit maturity notifications.
