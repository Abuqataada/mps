Rigorous Project Selection Process

All MPS investment projects undergo a comprehensive due diligence process including market analysis, risk assessment, feasibility studies, and ROI projections. We prioritize projects with strong growth potential, social impact, and sustainable returns for our investors.

Flexible Investment Options

Our micro mutual funds start from ₦5,000, making them accessible to everyone. Real estate projects typically require a minimum of ₦500,000. We offer various investment tiers to accommodate different budget levels while ensuring optimal returns.

Transparent Return Distribution

Returns are distributed according to each project's specific terms. Micro funds provide daily interest accessible after 20 working days. Real estate projects distribute returns quarterly or annually. All distributions are automated and visible in your dashboard with detailed transaction history.
